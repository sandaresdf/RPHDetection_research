## 1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

This policy establishes standardized procedures for document creation, management, retention, and disposal across TechVenture Solutions. All employees, contractors, and third-party partners are required to comply with these guidelines when handling company documents in any format, including electronic files, physical records, and multimedia content.

## 2. DOCUMENT NAMING CONVENTIONS

### 2.1 Standard Format
All documents must follow the naming convention: **[Department]_[DocumentType]_[Subject]_[YYYY-MM-DD]_[Version]**

Examples:
- `HR_Policy_RemoteWork_2024-03-15_v1.0`
- `ENG_Specification_APIGateway_2024-02-28_v2.3`
- `SALES_Proposal_ClientABC_2024-03-10_v1.1`

### 2.2 Department Abbreviations
- HR: Human Resources
- ENG: Engineering
- SALES: Sales and Marketing
- FIN: Finance
- OPS: Operations
- LEGAL: Legal and Compliance

### 2.3 Prohibited Characters
File names must not contain: `< > : " | ? * \ /` or exceed 255 characters total length.

### 2.4 Special Categories
- Meeting minutes: `MEETING_[Department]_[YYYY-MM-DD]_[Purpose]`
- Contracts: `CONTRACT_[ClientName]_[ContractType]_[YYYY-MM-DD]`
- Financial records: `FIN_[RecordType]_[Period]_[YYYY-MM-DD]`

## 3. VERSION CONTROL PROCEDURES

### 3.1 Version Numbering System
- **Major versions:** Increment whole number (1.0, 2.0, 3.0) for substantial content changes
- **Minor versions:** Increment decimal (1.1, 1.2, 1.3) for revisions, corrections, or additions
- **Draft versions:** Use "DRAFT" suffix until final approval

### 3.2 Version Control Responsibilities
Document authors must:
- Maintain a revision log within each document
- Track changes using the SharePoint version history feature
- Notify stakeholders via email when new versions are published
- Archive previous versions in the designated "Archive" folder

### 3.3 Approval Workflow
1. **Draft Stage:** Author creates initial document with "DRAFT" designation
2. **Review Stage:** Department head reviews and provides feedback within 5 business days
3. **Approval Stage:** Designated approver signs off electronically via DocuSign
4. **Publication Stage:** Final version uploaded to shared drive with proper naming convention

### 3.4 Change Documentation
All version changes must include:
- Date of modification
- Author of changes
- Summary of modifications
- Approval authorization

## 4. RETENTION SCHEDULES

### 4.1 Document Categories and Retention Periods

**Category A - Permanent Retention:**
- Articles of incorporation and bylaws
- Patent and trademark documentation
- Audit reports and tax returns
- Board meeting minutes and resolutions

**Category B - Long-term Retention (7 years):**
- Financial statements and general ledgers
- Employee personnel files (post-termination)
- Contracts and legal agreements
- Insurance policies and claims

**Category C - Medium-term Retention (3 years):**
- Project documentation and specifications
- Vendor invoices and purchase orders
- Marketing materials and campaigns
- Training records and certifications

**Category D - Short-term Retention (1 year):**
- Routine correspondence and emails
- Meeting notes and informal documentation
- Temporary project files
- System logs and backup files

### 4.2 Retention Tracking
The Operations team maintains a master retention schedule database accessible at `\\shared\ops\retention_tracking.xlsx`. Department heads must review and update retention classifications quarterly.

## 5. ARCHIVAL PROCEDURES

### 5.1 Electronic Document Archival
Documents reaching retention milestones are automatically moved to the TechVenture Archive System (TAS) via scheduled scripts running monthly. The archive system maintains:
- Original file format preservation
- Searchable metadata indexing
- Access control based on document sensitivity
- Backup redundancy across multiple data centers

### 5.2 Physical Document Archival
Physical documents requiring archival are processed as follows:
1. **Scanning:** Convert to PDF format using department scanners
2. **Quality Control:** Verify legibility and completeness
3. **Digital Storage:** Upload to TAS with appropriate metadata
4. **Physical Storage:** Secure in designated storage room (Building A, Room 105)
5. **Inventory Update:** Log in physical inventory database

### 5.3 Access to Archived Documents
Archived document requests must be submitted via the Document Request Form (Form OPS-DR-001) to operations@techventure.com. Standard retrieval time is 2-3 business days for electronic archives and 5-7 business days for physical archives.

## 6. DOCUMENT DISPOSAL

### 6.1 Secure Disposal Requirements
Documents containing sensitive information require secure disposal through:
- **Electronic:** Certified data wiping using NIST 800-88 standards
- **Physical:** Cross-cut shredding by authorized vendor SecureShred Inc.

### 6.2 Disposal Authorization
Document disposal requires written authorization from:
- Department head for routine documents
- Legal department for contracts and agreements
- CFO for financial records
- CISO for documents containing technical specifications

### 6.3 Disposal Documentation
All disposal activities are logged in the Disposal Tracking System, recording document type, disposal date, method used, and authorizing personnel.

## 7. COMPLIANCE AND ENFORCEMENT

Failure to comply with this policy may result in disciplinary action. Department heads are responsible for ensuring team compliance and conducting annual policy reviews. Questions regarding document management should be directed to the Operations Help Desk at ext. 2150 or operations-help@techventure.com.
