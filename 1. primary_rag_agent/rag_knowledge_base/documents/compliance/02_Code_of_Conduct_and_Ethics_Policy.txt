## 1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

This Code of Conduct and Ethics Policy establishes the fundamental principles and behavioral standards that govern all employees, contractors, consultants, and board members of TechVenture Solutions ("Company"). This policy ensures our organization maintains the highest standards of integrity, professionalism, and ethical conduct in all business activities.

All personnel are required to acknowledge receipt and understanding of this policy through the ComplianceTracker system within 30 days of hire or policy updates.

## 2. PROFESSIONAL BEHAVIOR STANDARDS

### 2.1 Workplace Conduct

All Company personnel must maintain professional demeanor and conduct that reflects positively on TechVenture Solutions' reputation. This includes:

- **Respectful Communication:** Use professional language in all interactions, whether verbal, written, or electronic. Discriminatory, harassing, or threatening language is strictly prohibited.
- **Punctuality and Reliability:** Attend meetings promptly, meet deadlines, and fulfill commitments to colleagues and clients.
- **Collaborative Approach:** Foster an inclusive environment that values diverse perspectives and promotes teamwork.

### 2.2 Client and Vendor Relations

When representing TechVenture Solutions externally, employees must:

1. Present accurate information about Company capabilities and services
2. Honor all contractual commitments and service level agreements
3. Address client concerns promptly through proper channels
4. Maintain professional appearance during client-facing activities

### 2.3 Digital Communications and Social Media

Professional behavior extends to all digital platforms:

- Company email systems are for business purposes; personal use should be minimal and appropriate
- Social media posts referencing TechVenture Solutions must align with Company messaging guidelines
- Confidential information must never be shared through personal social media accounts
- Report suspected phishing or security threats to security@techventure.com immediately

### 2.4 Violation Reporting

Employees witnessing unprofessional conduct should report incidents through:
- Direct supervisor escalation
- Ethics Hotline: 1-800-ETHICS-1
- Anonymous reporting via ethics@techventure.com
- ComplianceTracker incident reporting module

## 3. CONFLICTS OF INTEREST

### 3.1 Definition and Identification

A conflict of interest occurs when personal interests interfere with, or appear to interfere with, the Company's interests. Common scenarios include:

- Financial interests in competing organizations or suppliers
- Employment or consulting relationships with competitors
- Personal relationships affecting business decisions
- Use of Company resources for personal benefit

### 3.2 Disclosure Requirements

All potential conflicts must be disclosed using Form COI-2024 through the following process:

1. **Initial Disclosure:** Submit completed form to compliance@techventure.com within 10 business days of identifying the conflict
2. **Management Review:** Direct supervisor and Compliance Officer evaluate the situation
3. **Resolution Plan:** If conflict exists, develop mitigation strategy or recusal procedures
4. **Documentation:** All decisions recorded in employee compliance file

### 3.3 Common Conflict Scenarios

**Outside Employment:** Employees may not work for competitors, suppliers, or clients without written approval. Part-time consulting must be disclosed if it involves technology sector activities.

**Financial Investments:** Holdings exceeding 5% in vendor companies or any investment in direct competitors requires disclosure and potential divestiture.

**Family Relationships:** When family members work for business partners or competitors, employees must recuse themselves from related decision-making processes.

### 3.4 Annual Certification

All employees at Manager level and above must complete annual conflict of interest certifications by December 31st through the ComplianceTracker system.

## 4. GIFT POLICY

### 4.1 General Principles

TechVenture Solutions prohibits accepting gifts that could influence business decisions or create obligations to external parties. This policy applies to all forms of value including meals, entertainment, travel, and promotional items.

### 4.2 Acceptable Gifts

The following items are generally acceptable without approval:

- Promotional items with vendor logos valued under $25
- Occasional business meals under $75 per person
- Industry conference materials and standard hospitality
- Holiday gifts with nominal value (under $50)

### 4.3 Prohibited Gifts

Never accept:

- Cash or cash equivalents (gift cards, vouchers, etc.)
- Personal services or favors
- Entertainment at exclusive venues or private events
- Travel expenses or accommodations
- Gifts to family members from business associates

### 4.4 Gift Approval Process

Gifts exceeding acceptable limits require pre-approval:

1. **Request Submission:** Complete Form GFT-APP-2024 describing the gift, value, and business context
2. **Supervisor Review:** Direct manager evaluates appropriateness and business justification
3. **Compliance Approval:** Values over $200 require additional Compliance Officer approval
4. **Documentation:** Approved gifts logged in GiftTracker database

**Example:** A software vendor invites the IT Director to a professional sports event. The ticket value ($300) exceeds policy limits, requiring pre-approval through the gift approval process.

### 4.5 Gift Giving Guidelines

When representing TechVenture Solutions, employees may offer modest gifts following these guidelines:

- Business-related items (Company-branded materials, industry publications)
- Cultural courtesy gifts when conducting international business
- Maximum value of $100 without executive approval
- Document all gifts over $50 in expense reports

## 5. CONFIDENTIALITY

### 5.1 Information Classification

TechVenture Solutions classifies information into four categories:

- **Public:** Information available to general public
- **Internal:** Standard business information for employee use
- **Confidential:** Sensitive information requiring protection
- **Restricted:** Highly sensitive information with limited access

### 5.2 Confidential Information Protection

Employees must safeguard:

- Proprietary software code and development methodologies
- Client data and project specifications
- Financial information and business strategies
- Personnel records and compensation details
- Merger and acquisition discussions

### 5.3 Information Handling Procedures

**Physical Security:**
- Lock confidential documents when unattended
- Use privacy screens in open work areas
- Dispose of sensitive materials through designated shredding services
- Restrict access to authorized personnel only

**Digital Security:**
- Classify all documents using the InfoGuard system
- Encrypt confidential files using Company-approved tools
- Access confidential systems only through secure VPN connections
- Report data breaches to security@techventure.com within 2 hours

### 5.4 Third-Party Disclosures

Before sharing confidential information externally:

1. Verify recipient has signed appropriate Non-Disclosure Agreement
2. Obtain approval from Information Security Officer for Confidential data
3. Require executive approval for Restricted information sharing
4. Document all disclosures in the DataShare tracking system

### 5.5 Post-Employment Obligations

Confidentiality obligations continue after employment termination. Former employees must:

- Return all Company information and devices
- Refrain from using or disclosing confidential information
- Honor non-compete and non-solicitation agreements
- Report any inadvertent disclosures to legal@techventure.com

## 6. POLICY ENFORCEMENT AND COMPLIANCE

### 6.1 Training Requirements

All employees must complete:
- Initial ethics training within 30 days of hire
- Annual refresher training through LearningHub system
- Specialized training for roles handling sensitive information

### 6.2 Monitoring and Auditing

The Compliance Office conducts regular assessments including:
- Quarterly conflict of interest reviews
- Annual gift policy compliance audits
- Random confidentiality procedure evaluations

### 6.3 Consequences of Violations

Policy violations may result in:
- Verbal or written warnings
- Mandatory additional training
- Suspension or termination of employment
- Legal action for severe breaches
