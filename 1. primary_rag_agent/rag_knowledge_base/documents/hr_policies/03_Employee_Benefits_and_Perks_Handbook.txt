## 1. INTRODUCTION AND OVERVIEW

TechVenture Solutions is committed to providing a comprehensive benefits package that supports the health, financial security, and professional growth of our employees. This handbook outlines the benefits and perks available to eligible employees, including detailed information about enrollment procedures, coverage details, and administrative requirements.

All full-time employees (32+ hours per week) are eligible for the complete benefits package. Part-time employees (20-31 hours per week) are eligible for select benefits as indicated throughout this document. Temporary contractors and interns are not eligible for benefits coverage unless specifically noted.

## 2. HEALTH INSURANCE PROGRAMS

### 2.1 Medical Insurance Coverage

TechVenture Solutions partners with BlueCross BlueShield to provide comprehensive medical insurance options through our Benefits Administration Portal (BAP).

**Available Plans:**
- **Premium Plan (PPO):** $0 employee contribution for individual coverage; $180/month for family coverage
- **Standard Plan (HMO):** $45/month individual; $220/month family
- **High-Deductible Health Plan (HDHP):** $25/month individual; $165/month family

**Enrollment Requirements:**
1. Complete Form HR-101 (Health Insurance Election) within 30 days of hire date
2. Submit dependent verification documents to benefits@techventure.com
3. Attend mandatory benefits orientation session (scheduled through Workday system)

**Coverage Effective Date:** First day of the month following 30 days of employment

### 2.2 Dental and Vision Insurance

**Dental Coverage (Delta Dental):**
- Preventive care: 100% coverage
- Basic procedures: 80% coverage after $50 deductible
- Major procedures: 50% coverage after deductible
- Employee contribution: $15/month individual, $35/month family

**Vision Coverage (VSP):**
- Annual eye exam: $10 copay
- Frames allowance: $150 every 24 months
- Contact lens allowance: $120 annually
- Employee contribution: $8/month individual, $18/month family

### 2.3 Supplemental Health Benefits

**Health Savings Account (HSA):** Available to HDHP participants with annual company contribution of $500 (individual) or $1,000 (family)

**Flexible Spending Account (FSA):** Pre-tax contribution up to $3,050 annually for healthcare expenses

**Life Insurance:** Company-provided term life insurance equal to 2x annual salary, with option to purchase additional coverage up to $500,000

## 3. RETIREMENT AND FINANCIAL BENEFITS

### 3.1 401(k) Retirement Plan

TechVenture Solutions sponsors a 401(k) plan administered by Fidelity Investments, featuring:

**Company Matching:**
- 100% match on first 3% of employee contribution
- 50% match on next 2% of employee contribution
- Maximum annual company match: 4% of eligible compensation

**Vesting Schedule:**
- Years 1-2: 20% vested per year
- Year 3: 60% vested
- Year 4: 80% vested
- Year 5+: 100% vested

**Enrollment Process:**
1. Access Fidelity NetBenefits portal at www.netbenefits.com/techventure
2. Complete investment elections using Target Date Funds or self-directed options
3. Automatic enrollment at 3% contribution rate for new hires (opt-out available)

### 3.2 Stock Option Program

Eligible employees may participate in the Employee Stock Ownership Plan (ESOP) based on the following criteria:

**Eligibility Requirements:**
- Minimum 12 months of continuous employment
- Performance rating of "Meets Expectations" or higher
- Approval from direct supervisor and HR Director

**Grant Schedule:** Annual grants typically awarded in Q1 based on individual performance and company objectives

## 4. WELLNESS PROGRAMS AND INITIATIVES

### 4.1 Corporate Wellness Program

The "TechFit Wellness Initiative" promotes employee health through various programs and incentives:

**Wellness Portal Access:** Employees receive access to the Vitality wellness platform featuring:
- Health risk assessments and biometric screenings
- Fitness tracking integration (Fitbit, Apple Health, etc.)
- Nutrition counseling and meal planning resources
- Mental health support through Employee Assistance Program (EAP)

**Annual Wellness Credits:** Earn up to $300 in wellness credits by completing:
- Annual physical exam (75 credits)
- Preventive screenings (50 credits)
- Fitness challenges (100 credits)
- Smoking cessation program completion (75 credits)

### 4.2 On-Site Wellness Facilities

**Corporate Fitness Center:** Available 24/7 with keycard access, featuring:
- Cardiovascular equipment
- Weight training stations
- Group fitness classes (yoga, HIIT, cycling)
- Locker rooms and shower facilities

**Wellness Room:** Dedicated space for meditation, nursing mothers, and health consultations

### 4.3 Mental Health and Work-Life Balance

**Employee Assistance Program (EAP):** Confidential counseling services through ComPsych, offering:
- 24/7 crisis support hotline: 1-800-EAP-HELP
- Up to 8 free counseling sessions per issue, per year
- Legal and financial consultation services
- Work-life balance resources

**Flexible Work Arrangements:** Eligible employees may request remote work or flexible scheduling through Form HR-205, subject to manager approval and business needs assessment.

## 5. PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT OPPORTUNITIES

### 5.1 Tuition Reimbursement Program

TechVenture Solutions supports continuous learning through comprehensive tuition assistance:

**Eligibility Criteria:**
- Minimum 12 months of employment
- Satisfactory performance rating
- Course relevance to current role or career advancement within company

**Reimbursement Structure:**
- Undergraduate coursework: Up to $3,000 per calendar year
- Graduate coursework: Up to $5,000 per calendar year
- Professional certifications: Up to $2,000 per calendar year

**Application Process:**
1. Submit Form HR-301 (Educational Assistance Request) 30 days prior to course start
2. Obtain approval from direct manager and Learning & Development team
3. Maintain minimum grade of "B" (or equivalent) for reimbursement eligibility
4. Submit reimbursement request with official transcripts within 60 days of course completion

### 5.2 Internal Training and Development

**Technical Skills Development:**
- Annual training budget of $1,500 per employee for conferences, workshops, and online learning platforms
- Access to LinkedIn Learning, Pluralsight, and Coursera for Business
- Internal "Tech Talks" series featuring industry experts and thought leaders

**Leadership Development Program:** High-potential employees may participate in our 12-month leadership track, including:
- Executive coaching sessions
- Cross-functional project assignments
- Mentorship pairing with senior leadership
- External leadership conference attendance

### 5.3 Career Advancement Support

**Internal Mobility Program:** Priority consideration for internal candidates on job postings, with dedicated career counseling through careers@techventure.com

**Professional Membership Reimbursement:** Up to $500 annually for relevant professional association memberships and industry certifications

## 6. ADDITIONAL PERKS AND BENEFITS

### 6.1 Time-Off Benefits

**Paid Time Off (PTO):** Accrual-based system with immediate eligibility:
- Years 1-3: 15 days annually
- Years 4-7: 20 days annually  
- Years 8+: 25 days annually

**Holiday Schedule:** 12 paid holidays plus 2 floating personal days

**Parental Leave:** 12 weeks paid leave for birth or adoption of child, supplementing state benefits

### 6.2 Workplace Amenities

- Complimentary beverages, snacks, and catered lunch on Fridays
- Reserved parking for carpools and electric vehicles
- Commuter benefits program with pre-tax transit passes
- Technology allowance for home office setup ($750 for remote employees)
