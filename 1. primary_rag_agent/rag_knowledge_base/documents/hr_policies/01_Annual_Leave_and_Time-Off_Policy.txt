## 1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

This policy establishes guidelines for annual leave entitlement, approval procedures, and time-off management for all TechVenture Solutions employees. This policy applies to all full-time and part-time permanent employees across all departments and geographic locations.

## 2. LEAVE ENTITLEMENT

### 2.1 Annual Leave Allocation

Annual leave entitlement is based on length of service and employment status:

**Full-Time Employees:**
- Years 0-2: 15 working days per calendar year
- Years 3-5: 20 working days per calendar year  
- Years 6-10: 25 working days per calendar year
- Years 11+: 30 working days per calendar year

**Part-Time Employees:**
- Pro-rated based on scheduled working hours relative to full-time equivalent (37.5 hours per week)

### 2.2 Leave Accrual Method

- New employees accrue leave monthly at 1/12th of their annual entitlement
- Leave begins accruing from the first day of employment
- Employees may utilize accrued leave after completing 90 days of employment
- Leave entitlement increases on the anniversary of the employee's start date

### 2.3 Additional Leave Categories

**Personal Days:** All employees receive 3 personal days annually, separate from annual leave allocation.

**Bereavement Leave:** Up to 5 days for immediate family members (spouse, children, parents, siblings), 3 days for extended family members.

## 3. LEAVE APPROVAL PROCESS

### 3.1 Standard Approval Workflow

1. **Request Submission:** Employees must submit leave requests through the PeopleForce HR system at least 10 business days in advance for planned leave
2. **Manager Review:** Direct supervisors have 3 business days to approve or decline requests
3. **HR Notification:** Approved requests automatically generate notifications to hr-operations@techventure.com
4. **Calendar Integration:** Approved leave syncs with Outlook calendars and team scheduling systems

### 3.2 Emergency Leave Requests

For unplanned absences due to illness or emergency:
- Contact direct supervisor within 2 hours of normal start time
- Submit formal request in PeopleForce system within 48 hours of return
- Provide supporting documentation if absence exceeds 3 consecutive days

### 3.3 Approval Authority Matrix

| Leave Duration | Approval Required |
|----------------|-------------------|
| 1-5 days | Direct Manager |
| 6-10 days | Department Head |
| 11+ days | Department Head + HR Director |

### 3.4 Denial and Appeals Process

Managers may deny leave requests based on:
- Business critical periods (product launches, quarter-end, etc.)
- Insufficient staffing coverage
- Previously approved team member absences

Employees may appeal denials through hr-appeals@techventure.com within 5 business days.

## 4. CARRYOVER RULES

### 4.1 Annual Carryover Limits

- **Maximum Carryover:** Employees may carry forward up to 5 unused annual leave days to the following calendar year
- **Use-It Period:** Carried-over days must be utilized by March 31st of the subsequent year
- **Forfeiture:** Any carried-over days not used by the deadline are forfeited without compensation

### 4.2 Carryover Approval Process

- Automatic carryover applies for eligible unused days up to the 5-day limit
- Employees with exceptional circumstances may request additional carryover through the Exception Request Form (HR-EX-001)
- Exception requests require Department Head and HR Director approval
- Maximum exception carryover: 10 days (requires CEO approval)

### 4.3 Leave Balance Notifications

The PeopleForce system generates automated reminders:
- November 1st: Annual leave balance notification
- December 1st: Final reminder for year-end leave planning
- January 15th: Carryover balance confirmation
- March 15th: Final reminder for carried-over day utilization

## 5. PUBLIC HOLIDAYS

### 5.1 Recognized Public Holidays

TechVenture Solutions observes the following public holidays:
- New Year's Day
- Martin Luther King Jr. Day
- Presidents' Day
- Memorial Day
- Independence Day
- Labor Day
- Thanksgiving Day
- Day after Thanksgiving
- Christmas Eve
- Christmas Day
- New Year's Eve

**Total:** 11 company-recognized holidays annually

### 5.2 Holiday Pay and Scheduling

- Full-time employees receive regular pay for recognized holidays
- Part-time employees receive holiday pay proportional to scheduled hours
- When holidays fall on weekends, the company observes the federal substitute day
- Employees required to work on holidays receive holiday premium pay (1.5x regular rate) plus compensatory time off

### 5.3 Floating Holidays

Employees receive 2 floating holiday days annually to accommodate personal religious or cultural observances not covered by standard company holidays. Floating holidays:
- Must be used within the calendar year (no carryover permitted)
- Require 5 business days advance notice
- Follow standard approval workflow

## 6. IMPLEMENTATION AND COMPLIANCE

### 6.1 Manager Responsibilities

- Review and respond to leave requests within specified timeframes
- Ensure adequate coverage planning for approved absences
- Monitor team leave patterns to prevent operational disruptions
- Maintain confidentiality regarding employee leave reasons

### 6.2 Employee Responsibilities

- Submit requests through proper channels and timeframes
- Provide adequate project handover documentation before extended leave
- Update out-of-office messages and delegate urgent responsibilities
- Notify HR of any changes to approved leave plans

### 6.3 Policy Violations

Failure to comply with this policy may result in disciplinary action, including:
- Written warnings for repeated late submissions
- Leave request denial for policy non-compliance
- Progressive discipline for unauthorized absences
