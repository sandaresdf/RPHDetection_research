## 1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

This document establishes standardized procedures for identifying, reporting, responding to, and managing information security incidents at TechVenture Solutions. All employees, contractors, and third-party vendors with access to TechVenture systems, data, or facilities must comply with these procedures to ensure rapid containment, investigation, and resolution of security events.

## 2. DEFINITIONS

**Security Incident:** Any event that compromises or potentially compromises the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of TechVenture's information assets, systems, or services.

**Security Event:** An observable occurrence in a system or network that may indicate a security incident but requires further analysis for confirmation.

**Incident Response Team (IRT):** Designated personnel responsible for coordinating and executing incident response activities.

**Critical Incident:** High-impact security incident requiring immediate executive notification and potentially external regulatory reporting.

## 3. INCIDENT CLASSIFICATION AND TYPES

### 3.1 Severity Levels

**CRITICAL (P1):** Incidents causing severe business disruption, data breach involving PII/PHI, ransomware deployment, or complete system compromise affecting core business operations.

**HIGH (P2):** Incidents with significant business impact, unauthorized access to sensitive systems, malware infections on multiple systems, or suspected insider threats.

**MEDIUM (P3):** Incidents with moderate business impact, isolated malware infections, policy violations, or suspicious activities requiring investigation.

**LOW (P4):** Minor incidents with minimal business impact, failed login attempts, or routine security events requiring documentation.

### 3.2 Incident Categories

#### 3.2.1 Cyber Security Incidents
- Malware infections (viruses, trojans, ransomware)
- Unauthorized network access or intrusions
- Phishing and social engineering attacks
- Denial of Service (DoS) attacks
- Data exfiltration or theft
- Account compromises and credential theft

#### 3.2.2 Data Security Incidents
- Unauthorized disclosure of confidential information
- Data corruption or loss
- Privacy breaches involving customer or employee data
- Improper data handling or storage violations

#### 3.2.3 Physical Security Incidents
- Unauthorized facility access
- Theft or loss of computing devices
- Tampering with physical security controls
- Environmental threats affecting IT infrastructure

#### 3.2.4 Operational Security Incidents
- System outages due to security events
- Third-party vendor security breaches affecting TechVenture
- Insider threat activities
- Security control failures

## 4. REPORTING CHANNELS AND PROCEDURES

### 4.1 Primary Reporting Methods

**Security Operations Center (SOC):** Available 24/7/365
- Phone: +1-555-SEC-HELP (732-4357)
- Email: security-incidents@techventure.com
- Internal Portal: ServiceNow Security Incident Module

**IT Service Desk:** Business hours (7:00 AM - 7:00 PM EST)
- Phone: +1-555-IT-SUPPORT
- Email: helpdesk@techventure.com
- Slack Channel: #security-alerts

### 4.2 Reporting Requirements

All personnel must report suspected security incidents within **15 minutes** of discovery for Critical incidents and **2 hours** for all other incidents. Reports must include:

1. **Initial Assessment Form (IAF-SEC-001)** completed via ServiceNow
2. Date, time, and method of discovery
3. Affected systems, applications, or data
4. Preliminary impact assessment
5. Actions already taken
6. Contact information of reporting individual

### 4.3 Anonymous Reporting

Employees may report security concerns anonymously through:
- Ethics Hotline: +1-800-ETHICS-1
- Online Portal: https://ethics.techventure.com
- Physical suggestion boxes located in break rooms

- Chief Executive Officer: Patricia Johnson

### 6.2 External Escalation Requirements

**Law Enforcement**
- FBI Cyber Division: For criminal activities, nation-state attacks
- Local Police: For physical security incidents with criminal elements
- **Authorization Required:** Legal Counsel approval

**Regulatory Bodies**
- State Attorney General: Data breaches (within 72 hours)
- Industry Regulators: Sector-specific requirements
- Cyber Insurance Provider: All P1/P2 incidents
- **Timeline:** Per regulatory requirements

**Customer/Partner Notification**
- Affected customers: Within 24 hours of confirmation
- Strategic partners: Within 12 hours for service impacts
- **Method:** Formal notification via customer portal and email

### 6.3 Escalation Decision Criteria

Automatic escalation triggers:
- Any incident affecting >100 users
- Suspected data breach involving customer data
- Media attention or public disclosure
- Ransom demands or extortion attempts
- Regulatory violation indicators
- Estimated financial impact >$50,000

## 7. DOCUMENTATION AND FOLLOW-UP

### 7.1 Required Documentation

All incidents require completion of:
- **Incident Response Log (IRL-SEC-001):** Real-time activity tracking
- **Post-Incident Review Report (PIR-SEC-001):** Within 5 business days
- **Lessons Learned Summary (LLS-SEC-001):** Within 10 business days

### 7.2 Evidence Preservation

Digital forensics procedures follow NIST SP 800-86 guidelines:
- Chain of custody documentation (Form COC-SEC-001)
- Forensic imaging using FTK Imager or EnCase
- Evidence storage in secure facility (Building A, Room 150)
- Access logging via RFID badge system

### 7.3 Continuous Improvement

Monthly incident metrics reviewed by IT Security Committee including:
- Mean Time to Detection (MTTD)
- Mean Time to Containment (MTTC)
- Mean Time to Recovery (MTTR)
- Incident recurrence rates
- Training effectiveness indicators

## 8. TRAINING AND AWARENESS

All incident response team members must complete:
- SANS Incident Response certification (annual renewal)
- TechVenture tabletop exercises (quarterly)
- Industry threat briefings (monthly)
- Secure communications training using Signal and ProtonMail

Regular organization-wide security awareness training includes incident reporting procedures and is tracked via the TechVenture Learning Management System (LMS).
