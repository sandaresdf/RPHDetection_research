## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

TechVenture Solutions recognizes that information assets constitute the foundation of our competitive advantage and operational excellence. This Data Classification and Handling Policy establishes mandatory frameworks for categorizing, protecting, storing, sharing, and disposing of all data assets within our organization. Compliance with this policy ensures regulatory adherence, mitigates security risks, and preserves client confidentiality while enabling appropriate business functionality.

## 2. SCOPE AND APPLICABILITY

### 2.1 Coverage
This policy applies to all TechVenture Solutions personnel, including:
- Full-time and part-time employees
- Contractors and temporary staff
- Third-party vendors with data access
- Board members and consultants
- Remote workers and distributed teams

### 2.2 Data Types Covered
All information in any format, including but not limited to:
- Electronic files and databases
- Physical documents and printouts
- Audio and video recordings
- Backup and archived data
- Metadata and system logs
- Communications and correspondence

## 3. DATA CLASSIFICATION FRAMEWORK

### 3.1 Classification Levels

#### 3.1.1 CONFIDENTIAL (Level 4)
**Definition:** Information requiring the highest level of protection due to potential severe impact on TechVenture Solutions, clients, or individuals if disclosed.

**Examples:**
- Client proprietary algorithms and source code
- Financial projections and strategic plans
- Personnel records and salary information
- Security vulnerability assessments
- Merger and acquisition discussions

**Handling Requirements:**
- Encryption mandatory for all storage and transmission
- Access limited to named individuals with signed confidentiality agreements
- Physical documents stored in locked cabinets within secured areas
- Digital watermarking required for all electronic copies

#### 3.1.2 RESTRICTED (Level 3)
**Definition:** Sensitive business information requiring controlled access and protection from unauthorized disclosure.

**Examples:**
- Internal project specifications
- Vendor contracts and pricing agreements
- Customer contact databases
- Performance metrics and analytics
- Technical architecture documentation

**Handling Requirements:**
- Role-based access controls implemented
- Encryption required for external transmission
- Physical documents secured in designated areas
- Access logging and monitoring enabled

#### 3.1.3 INTERNAL (Level 2)
**Definition:** Information intended for internal use that could cause minor business disruption if inappropriately disclosed.

**Examples:**
- Company policies and procedures
- Internal training materials
- Organizational charts
- General project timelines
- Internal communications and announcements

**Handling Requirements:**
- Standard authentication required
- Sharing restricted to TechVenture Solutions personnel
- Basic physical security measures applied
- Email transmission permitted within organization

#### 3.1.4 PUBLIC (Level 1)
**Definition:** Information approved for public disclosure with no anticipated negative impact.

**Examples:**
- Marketing materials and press releases
- Published research papers
- Public website content
- Product documentation for released features
- Job postings and company announcements

**Handling Requirements:**
- Standard business practices apply
- No special protection measures required
- Appropriate for external sharing and publication

### 3.2 Classification Responsibilities

**Data Owners:** Department heads and senior managers responsible for initial classification decisions and periodic reviews.

**Data Custodians:** IT administrators and system managers responsible for implementing technical controls and access management.

**Data Users:** All personnel accessing or handling classified information, responsible for maintaining appropriate protection measures.

## 4. STORAGE REQUIREMENTS

### 4.1 Technical Storage Standards

#### 4.1.1 CONFIDENTIAL Data Storage
- **Encryption:** AES-256 encryption mandatory for all storage media
- **Location:** Approved enterprise systems only (SharePoint Confidential, SecureVault Pro)
- **Backup:** Encrypted backups stored in geographically separate facilities
- **Access Control:** Multi-factor authentication and privileged access management
- **Monitoring:** Continuous access monitoring with real-time alerting

#### 4.1.2 RESTRICTED Data Storage
- **Encryption:** AES-128 minimum encryption standard
- **Location:** Enterprise-approved systems (SharePoint Standard, ProjectHub Enterprise)
- **Backup:** Regular encrypted backups with 30-day retention minimum
- **Access Control:** Role-based permissions with quarterly access reviews
- **Monitoring:** Weekly access reports and anomaly detection

#### 4.1.3 INTERNAL Data Storage
- **Encryption:** Standard encryption for data at rest
- **Location:** Approved business systems and network drives
- **Backup:** Standard backup procedures with 90-day retention
- **Access Control:** Department-level permissions with annual reviews
- **Monitoring:** Monthly access audits and compliance reporting

### 4.2 Physical Storage Requirements

**CONFIDENTIAL and RESTRICTED** physical documents must be stored in:
- Fire-rated safes or security-rated filing cabinets
- Access-controlled rooms with badge authentication
- Areas monitored by security cameras
- Locations with environmental controls and intrusion detection

**INTERNAL** physical documents require:
- Locked filing cabinets or secured office areas
- Basic access controls during non-business hours
- Protection from environmental hazards

### 4.3 Cloud Storage Protocols

All cloud storage solutions must:
- Maintain SOC 2 Type II certification
- Provide data residency controls for applicable jurisdictions
- Implement zero-trust architecture principles
- Offer comprehensive audit logging and monitoring capabilities
- Execute Data Processing Agreements (DPAs) meeting GDPR requirements

Approved cloud platforms: Microsoft Azure Government, AWS GovCloud, TechVenture Private Cloud

## 5. SHARING PROTOCOLS

### 5.1 Internal Sharing Procedures

#### 5.1.1 Electronic Sharing
- **CONFIDENTIAL:** Encrypted email through SecureMail Pro with recipient verification required
- **RESTRICTED:** Standard corporate email with sensitivity headers
- **INTERNAL:** Corporate email systems and collaboration platforms
- **PUBLIC:** Any approved communication method

#### 5.1.2 Physical Document Sharing
- Hand-delivery with signed receipt acknowledgment
- Tracked courier services for external sharing
- Secure document destruction after use completion
- Prohibition of unattended documents in common areas

### 5.2 External Sharing Requirements

#### 5.2.1 Third-Party Sharing
All external sharing of CONFIDENTIAL or RESTRICTED data requires:
1. Executed Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) using template NDA-2024-STD
2. Completion of Data Sharing Request Form (DSRF-101)
3. Approval from Data Owner and Legal Department
4. Technical controls implementation (encryption, access limitations)
5. Sharing activity documentation in SharePoint Compliance Tracker

#### 5.2.2 Regulatory Compliance
Data sharing must comply with applicable regulations:
- **GDPR:** Lawful basis documentation and privacy impact assessments
- **CCPA:** Consumer rights notifications and opt-out mechanisms  
- **SOX:** Financial data controls and audit trail maintenance
- **HIPAA:** Healthcare information safeguards (where applicable)

### 5.3 Approval Workflows

**CONFIDENTIAL Data Sharing:**
1. Request submission to data-sharing@techventure.com
2. Legal review and risk assessment (5 business days)
3. CISO approval for technical controls
4. Executive sponsor sign-off
5. Implementation and monitoring setup

**RESTRICTED Data Sharing:**
1. Department manager approval
2. IT security controls verification
3. Legal review for external sharing
4. Documentation and tracking

## 6. DISPOSAL PROCEDURES

### 6.1 Electronic Data Disposal

#### 6.1.1 Secure Deletion Standards
- **CONFIDENTIAL:** DOD 5220.22-M standard (7-pass overwrite) or cryptographic erasure
- **RESTRICTED:** NIST SP 800-88 guidelines (3-pass overwrite minimum)
- **INTERNAL:** Single-pass overwrite with verification
- **PUBLIC:** Standard deletion procedures

#### 6.1.2 Storage Media Disposal
- Physical destruction of storage devices containing CONFIDENTIAL data
- Certified data destruction services with Certificate of Destruction
- Asset tracking through disposal completion
- Environmental compliance for electronic waste disposal

### 6.2 Physical Document Disposal

**CONFIDENTIAL and RESTRICTED documents:**
- Cross-cut shredding (security level P-4 minimum)
- Witnessed destruction with signed attestation
- Secure collection bins (locked and monitored)
- Professional destruction services for large volumes

**INTERNAL documents:**
- Standard cross-cut shredding (security level P-2 minimum)
- Supervised disposal procedures
- Regular collection and destruction schedules

### 6.3 Disposal Documentation

All disposal activities require:
- Completion of Data Disposal Form (DDF-2024)
- Supervisor approval and witnessing
- Certificate of Destruction retention (7 years minimum)
- Compliance audit trail maintenance
- Incident reporting for disposal irregularities

## 7. COMPLIANCE AND ENFORCEMENT

### 7.1 Monitoring and Auditing
- Quarterly compliance assessments conducted by Internal Audit
- Annual third-party security evaluations
- Continuous monitoring through SIEM platforms
- Random sampling and verification procedures

### 7.2 Violation Consequences
Policy violations may result in:
- Mandatory security awareness training
- Formal disciplinary action
- Termination of employment or contract
- Legal action for intentional breaches
- Financial liability for damages incurred

### 7.3 Training Requirements
All personnel must complete:
- Initial data classification training within 30 days of hire
- Annual refresher training and certification
- Role-specific advanced training for data custodians
- Incident response and breach notification procedures

## 8. POLICY REVIEW AND UPDATES

This policy undergoes annual review by the Information Security Committee, with updates triggered by:
- Regulatory changes or new compliance requirements
- Significant security incidents or threat landscape evolution
- Technology changes affecting data handling capabilities
- Business process modifications or organizational restructuring
